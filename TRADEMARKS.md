# The Project Prometheus name and logo

The code in this repository is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The name and the logo are not. Section 6 of the licence says it gives no permission to use them, and this page says what is and isn't allowed.

**What's covered:**
- the names **Project Prometheus** and **Prometheus 64**;
- the flame logo and app icons in [`assets/branding/`](assets/branding/), in every colour and variation.

The files in `assets/branding/` are © Wolfsbane Co., all rights reserved. The Apache License covers the rest of the repository, not them.

## Allowed

- **Sharing the official installer, unchanged and free of charge**, as downloaded from this repository's [Releases](https://github.com/WolfsbaneCo/project-prometheus/releases) page.
- **Talking about the app by name:** reviews, videos, guides, articles, links.
- **Building it from source for yourself**, unchanged, for your own use.

## Not allowed

- **Selling it, or charging for it in any way**, under these names or with this logo: paid downloads, paid bundles, paywalls, subscriptions, "donation required" downloads, ad-gated download sites, or preinstalled on devices that are sold.
- **Modified versions under these names or with this logo.** A fork must use its own name and its own icons, and must not say or suggest that it comes from, or is approved by, Wolfsbane Co.
- **Names or logos that could be mistaken for these.**
- **Using the name or logo to promote anything else**, or alongside game files, BIOS files or piracy of any kind.

## Reporting

To report a copy being sold or passed off as ours, open an issue on this repository.

Wolfsbane Co. may permit other uses in writing. Any use not listed above as allowed needs that permission.
