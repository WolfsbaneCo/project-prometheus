# Project Prometheus: privacy policy

*Last updated: 26 October 2026*

This policy covers Prometheus 64 and the other apps of Project Prometheus, made by Wolfsbane Company ("we"). In short: **we don't collect anything about you.** The apps run on your computer, your saves stay on your computer, and when you use online features, your data goes to accounts that you or your friends own, never to us.

## What stays on your computer

Your game library, settings, control layouts and saves stay on your own computer, in folders you can see. The apps never upload, download or look for game files.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose **Connect Google Drive**, you sign in with your own Google account, and the app asks Google for one permission: `drive.file`. That permission only covers files the app creates itself. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

- **What the app stores in your Drive:** a folder called "N64 Saves" with copies of your in-game saves and, if you leave it on, your save states. It also stores small files that record which save is newest and which of your computers made it (the computer's name).
- **What the app reads from your Drive:** only those same files, so another computer of yours can carry on from your latest save.
- **What we receive:** nothing. The files go straight between your computer and your Google Drive. We have no server in between and no access to your Drive.
- **Your sign-in:** Google gives the app a token so it can keep backing up without asking again. The token is stored only on your computer, in Windows Credential Manager (on other systems, in a file only your user account can read). We never receive it.
- **Stopping:** click **Disconnect** in the app to sign out; your files stay in your Drive until you delete them. You can also remove the app's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Project Prometheus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the backup feature you see in the app. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

## Online play (optional)

Online games go through a small relay on the Cloudflare account of whoever set up your group, not through us.

- **The Cloudflare key:** the group's owner pastes a Cloudflare key once, so the app can install the relay. The key is used for that setup only and is not stored.
- **What passes through the relay during a game:** the name each player types, controller inputs, connection timing, and, at the start of a game, the host's in-game save. The relay passes these between the players' apps and keeps nothing once the game ends.
- **The group key file:** it holds the relay's address and the group's key. You decide who you share it with.

## What we don't do

No accounts with us. No analytics, tracking or advertising. No selling or sharing of data. We don't receive your saves, your Google files or your game files.

## Children

The apps don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
